FAQ

Questions before you call

How many pages will my website have?

As many as your business needs. We recommend the pages after our first call, so you don't have to pick.

How quickly can my website be ready?

A standard site can be ready to launch in as little as 7 days once we have your details and account access. Have a deadline? Tell us on the call.

Which changes are included?

Routine content updates and small design adjustments. Bigger additions, redesigns and custom features get a price first.

What if I already have a website?

We'll look at it and tell you whether to improve it or start fresh.

Do you manage my Google Maps listing?

Yes. We set up or tidy your Google Business Profile at launch, then keep its hours, phone, website link and address accurate. Review management and local SEO are separate.

Do I own my website if I leave?

Yes. Your finished website belongs to your business, and if you move to another provider, we help with the handoff. Our own internal tools stay with us.
